Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

Trackability brings your study planning, focus and progress into one place.

What is Trackability?

Trackability is an all-in-one study tracking workspace. It brings together your daily tasks, focus sessions, syllabus progress, revision, MCQs, tests and personal study-related trackers so you can see what you planned, what you actually did and how your preparation is progressing.

Who is Trackability for?

Trackability is designed for students who want a structured way to plan and record their preparation. You can use only the sections that fit your routine, whether you mainly need a focus timer, syllabus tracking, daily tasks, revision tracking or a broader study dashboard.

How does the Focus Timer work?

You can start Study, Revision or Other focus sessions, choose a subject and chapter, and select a duration such as 25, 50, 90 minutes or a custom duration. The app also supports fullscreen timing, Pomodoro setup, focus history, daily goals and streak tracking.

Can I plan my study day and manage tasks?

Yes. The Tasks & Schedule Hub lets you organize your day and month using a calendar, task filters and a time-block timeline. Tasks can also be connected with activities such as MCQs, mistake review, gym or sleep, helping you keep your schedule in one place.

Can I track my syllabus and revision?

Yes. You can organize your syllabus by subjects and chapters and update chapter status as you progress. Chapters marked for revision can be brought into the Revision Hub, while chapters marked for MCQ work can appear in the MCQ Hub.

Can I track mistakes, MCQs and test performance?

Yes. Trackability includes a Mistake Book, MCQ ID Tracker and Grand Tests with score-trend tracking. These tools help you record practice and review your performance instead of relying only on the number of hours studied.

What can I track besides study time?

Along with study sessions, the app includes trackers for daily activity, gym, sleep, reviews and nutrition-related information such as protein and calories. You can use these alongside your study data to keep your routine organized.

Can I set an exam countdown and study goals?

Yes. The Home dashboard can show an exam countdown along with daily focus time, study goals, MCQs solved and completed-task progress. Focus goals, streaks and monthly goal tracking are also available in the Focus section.

Does Trackability save my progress?

Yes. The app uses local persistence for your app data and also includes account and cloud-sync functionality for signed-in users. The exact sync experience depends on the current app configuration.

Can I customize Trackability?

Yes. The app includes customization options such as themes and fonts, along with settings for organizing your workspace. You can also use Search to quickly find information across the app.

Does Trackability replace my usual study methods?

No. Trackability is meant to support the way you already study, not replace your books, classes, notes, question practice or other resources. It gives you a place to plan, time, record and review your preparation.
